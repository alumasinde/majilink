Questions

How much water can I order?

Between 1 and 20 jerrycans of 20 litres per order. You can set a weekly, fortnightly or monthly repeat delivery.

What if the vendor cannot make it?

You will see the order expire or be declined and can pick another nearby vendor. Nothing is charged for a declined order.

When do I pay?

Choose at checkout. Cash is paid on delivery; M-Pesa sends a prompt to your phone and the receipt stays on the order.

Is my exact address shared?

Only after a vendor accepts your order. Before that they see your first name, the quantity, the total and an approximate distance.
